# Dynamic Pricing Optimization System

## Overview
Optimize product pricing dynamically using demand elasticity, competition analysis, and profit maximization algorithms.

## Problem Statement
- Estimate price elasticity of demand
- Forecast demand at different price points
- Optimize prices to maximize profit
- Account for competitive pricing

## Key Features
- Price elasticity estimation
- Demand forecasting models
- Revenue/profit optimization
- Competitive pricing analysis

## Methodology
- Gradient Boosting for demand prediction
- Log-log regression for elasticity
- Constrained optimization for pricing
- Multi-factor demand modeling
