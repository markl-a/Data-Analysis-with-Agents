# Supply Chain Optimization System

## Overview
Comprehensive supply chain optimization system using demand forecasting and inventory management to minimize costs while maintaining high service levels.

## Problem Statement
Optimize supply chain operations to:
- Accurately forecast product demand
- Minimize inventory holding costs
- Reduce stockouts and lost sales
- Optimize ordering and logistics costs
- Maintain target service levels (>95%)

## Key Components

### 1. Demand Forecasting
- Time-series prediction with seasonality
- Multi-product forecasting
- Trend and pattern detection

### 2. Inventory Optimization
- Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
- Safety stock calculations
- Reorder point optimization

### 3. Cost Minimization
- Holding cost reduction
- Stockout cost minimization
- Ordering cost optimization

## Methodology
- Random Forest and Gradient Boosting for forecasting
- EOQ models for inventory optimization
- Service level constraint satisfaction
- Cost-benefit analysis

## Key Results
- Forecast MAPE: 10-15%
- Potential cost savings: 20-35%
- Service level: >95%
- Inventory turnover improvement: 30-40%
